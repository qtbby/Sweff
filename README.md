# Sweff — Simple Photo Framing Studio

**Live Demo:** [https://qtbby.github.io/Sweff/](https://qtbby.github.io/Sweff/)

Sweff is a beautifully simple, highly efficient batch photo framing web application designed to run entirely locally in your browser. It allows you to effortlessly combine your photos with custom portrait or landscape overlays (PNG frames) and export them.

## Features
- **Smart Frame Uploading**: Upload one Portrait and one Landscape frame simultaneously. The system intelligently detects and validates orientations to ensure no duplicates.
- **Auto-Matching**: Sweff automatically detects the orientation of your uploaded photos and matches them with the corresponding frame.
- **Fast, Clean Grid Preview**: See a neatly arranged CSS Grid preview of all your framed photos without overlaps.
- **Individual & Batch Editing**: Easily adjust crop, zoom, pan, and brightness for individual photos. Need the same adjustments? Hit the **Apply to All** button to sync settings across images of the same orientation!
- **Privacy First**: Everything runs on your local machine. No images are uploaded to external servers.
- **Multiple Themes**: Toggle seamlessly between Default (OS matched), Light, Dark, or Custom themes.
- **Buy Me a Coffee**: A fun, integrated way for users to support the developer via GCash, Maya, or PayPal.

## How to Use
1. **Open the Tool**: Visit the live demo link or open `index.html` locally.
2. **Upload Frames**: Select your transparent overlay PNGs (One portrait and one landscape max).
3. **Upload Photos**: Select all the photos you want to frame.
4. **Preview & Edit**: Click "Generate Preview". Click on "Edit" on any thumbnail to adjust its zoom, pan, and brightness.
5. **Download**: Name your batch and click "Download ZIP" to export all your beautiful high-resolution framed photos instantly.

## Requirements
- Any modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
- Internet connection only required to load CSS/JS CDNs (Tailwind, JSZip).
